Metric Score Description
Accuracy -- Percentage of all predictions correctly classified.
Precision -- Percentage of predicted positive cases that were positive.
Recall -- Percentage of actual positive cases identified.
F1 Score -- Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
ROC-AUC -- Model's ability to distinguish between classes.
Cross-validation F1 -- Mean F1 score across the training validation folds.
